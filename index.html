About

Where molecular biology meets biomedical AI

Biologist and MSc candidate in Molecular Biomedicine with a strong foundation in biomedical research and hands-on laboratory experience in neurogenetics and translational autoimmunity research. Alongside bench work, I'm building experience at the intersection of biology and artificial intelligence, using computational and machine-learning methods to turn biological and clinical data into interpretable insight.
